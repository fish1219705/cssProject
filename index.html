Why Protecting Water Resources Matters

Water is essential for life, yet our planet's resources are under constant threat. Learn how you can help preserve this vital resource for future generations.

Understanding Water Resources

What Are Water Resources?

Water resources include all forms of water—surface water, groundwater, and atmospheric water—that support life on Earth.

Threats to Our Water Resources

Overuse, pollution, and climate change are endangering our water supplies at an unprecedented rate.

Water Cycle Diagram

The Water Cycle

Evaporation: Water is heated and turns into vapor.

Condensation: Water vapor cools and forms clouds.

Precipitation: Water falls as rain.

Collection: Water gathers in lakes and rivers.

Suggestions for Saving Water

Fix leaks promptly to save gallons of water daily.

Use water-efficient appliances to reduce water usage.

Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth or washing dishes.
